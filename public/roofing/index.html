01 · Desert roof systems

What’s actually on—and
under—an Arizona roof?

The visible surface sheds sun and impact, while the underlayment and sheet metal underneath stop monsoon water. Open each system study for concrete questions to take to a licensed Arizona roofer. All images are illustrative architectural studies.

Concrete & clay S-tile / flat tileTypical underlayment cycle: 18–28 yrs

Clay & concrete tile roofs

Tiles frequently outlast the asphalt felt beneath them. A quote for swapping a few cracked tiles is fundamentally different from a full lift-and-relay that replaces dry, brittle underlayment, birdstop, battens, and valley metal.

Questions to ask on a tile bid
  • How many existing tiles are reused, what breakage percentage is included, and where are matched replacements sourced?
  • Is the underlayment single-ply, 2-ply 40-lb, or high-temperature SBS/synthetic rated for Phoenix deck heat?
  • Are new 1x2 treated battens, birdstop, and mortar or dry-vented ridge caps included in the base scope?
  • How is rotted OSB decking priced per sheet and documented before replacement?
W-valley metal · Battens · Pipe bootsHidden waterproof barrier

Underlayment, battens & valley flashing

Most Phoenix monsoon leaks do not begin in the middle of an open field—they start where roof planes meet in rusted valleys, cracked lead or rubber pipe boots, stucco wall transitions, and clogged eave channels.

Questions to ask about hidden layers
  • Will existing valley metal be replaced with new 26-gauge (or heavier) W-profile flashing rather than reused?
  • Are all plumbing pipe jacks, exhaust vents, and wall counterflashings replaced rather than smeared with mastic?
  • Will the contractor photograph the bare wood deck and completed dry-in before tiles or shingles cover them?
  • Are underlayment head-laps and double-layer valley linings installed to manufacturer specifications?
Class 3 / Class 4 laminated6-nail monsoon wind pattern

Architectural shingle roofs

Intense UV radiation and 150°F+ surface temperatures accelerate shingle granule loss and thermal brittleness. A solid proposal names exact shingle line, full tear-off to bare wood, starter strips, drip edge, and ventilation.

Questions to ask on a shingle bid
  • Does the price include full tear-off of all existing layers down to bare sheathing and roll-off disposal?
  • Which exact shingle line, starter strip, hip-and-ridge cap, and synthetic underlayment are specified?
  • Are shingles fastened with a 6-nail high-wind pattern for monsoon microburst uplift resistance?
  • How are intake soffit vents and exhaust ridge/static vents balanced so attic heat does not bake new shingles?
SPF foam · Silicone · 2-ply SBSDrainage & scupper critical

Low-slope, foam & parapet roofs

Flat patio tie-ins and territorial parapet roofs depend on clear scupper drainage and continuous elastomeric or modified-bitumen membranes. Coating over trapped moisture only seals a wet roof deck inside.

Questions to ask on a low-slope bid
  • Was the existing foam or membrane moisture-probed before recommending a recoat vs. full replacement?
  • How are ponding low spots, parapet wall terminations, and scupper outlets reinforced with fabric and flashing?
  • Is the topcoat high-solids silicone (for ponding tolerance) or acrylic elastomeric, and what dry mil thickness is specified?
  • What routine recoat maintenance interval does the finish require under Arizona sun?

02 · Structural cross-section

Six layers that keep
monsoon water outside.

When a contractor cuts corners to deliver the lowest price, the savings almost always come out of Layers 02 through 04—the hidden waterproof layers sealed underneath your tile or shingles.

  1. 01

    Structural deck & sheathing

    CDX plywood or OSB decking screwed/nailed to roof trusses. Water stains around valleys or eaves often hide delaminated sheets that must be photographed and swapped before dry-in.

  2. 02

    High-temp underlayment barrier

    The primary waterproof membrane on every Arizona tile roof. Heat-rated SBS modified or double-layer 40-lb systems resist 150°F+ summer roof-cavity baking far longer than thin single-ply felt.

  3. 03

    Valley, eave & wall flashings

    Heavy-gauge W-profile valley channels, continuous drip edge, eave riser metal, headwall flashing, and UV-resistant pipe boots direct high-volume storm runoff away from joints.

  4. 04

    Treated battens & birdstop

    Horizontal 1x2 batten strips elevate tiles slightly for airflow and water shedding underneath, while eave birdstop blocks desert birds and wind-blown debris from nesting under barrel tiles.

  5. 05

    Primary tile or shingle courses

    Concrete/clay tiles or Class 3/4 architectural shingles shield the underlying membrane from direct Sonoran UV rays, hail impact, and wind-driven debris.

  6. 06

    Ridge course & ventilation

    The finishing ridge cap seals the highest spine of the home. Properly mortared or dry-vented ridge courses pair with open soffit intake vents to exhaust trapped summer attic heat.

April – June Pre-Monsoon Inspection Window

Ideal time to clear valley debris, check cracked pipe boots, inspect ridge mortar, and verify low-slope scuppers before July storms arrive.

July – September Monsoon Microburst Watch

High-wind rain exposes aged 20-year tile underlayment and loose ridge tiles. Crews operate dawn shifts (5:00 AM starts) to avoid 110°F+ heat hazards.

October – March Prime Full-Replacement Season

Cooler deck temperatures allow predictable full tile lift-and-relays, shingle tear-offs, HOA approvals, and municipal inspections without storm backlogs.

03 · Interactive proposal scope auditor

Compare the work
behind the price.

Two written bids for the same Phoenix roof can sit $6,000 apart because one omits W-valley metal, permits, or hides per-sheet decking charges. Check off what appears in writing on up to two competing proposals below.

What comes off and what goes back?

Compare measured square counts, full tear-off and haul-away, named underlayment brand/plies, valley and pipe-boot flashing metal, and any work the bidder expects you to arrange separately (such as solar panel detach/reset).

What happens if hidden wood decking is damaged?

Require an exact per-sheet OSB/plywood rate and per-linear-foot fascia rate in the base contract—plus a written rule that no extra wood is billed without bare-deck photos and your approval first.

When are payments due & who pulls permits?

Tie every payment draw to a verifiable physical milestone (never 100% upfront). Confirm the bidder's active AZ ROC number, who pulls required municipal permits, and that Arizona's 3-business-day cancellation notice is attached.

What records will I own at handover?

Ask for bare-deck and dry-in photos, final permit sign-off, manufacturer material warranty registration, the contractor's separate workmanship warranty, and statutory supplier/subcontractor lien waivers.
