How it works

From a shared folder
to an office handoff.

Start with a single non-claim file. Your office keeps control of the originals, the decisions and every outside communication.

  1. 01

    Agree on the file

    Tell us the business and job states, the records involved and who needs the result. We screen the purpose before requesting any files.

  2. 02

    Set the fixed fee

    We agree on one job alias, the document limit, delivery date, recipient and handling terms in writing.

  3. 03

    Share redacted copies

    After acceptance, use the agreed restricted folder. Keep your originals and remove personal, banking, tax and claim information.

  4. 04

    Build the index

    We copy identifiers as supplied, link matching records and flag blanks or differences. Each entry points back to its source.

  5. 05

    Resolve office questions

    Your team decides what a difference means. We can correct a transcription error or add a label your staff supplies.

  6. 06

    Hand back the file

    Your office receives the index and open-items list. We close access and delete working copies on the agreed schedule.

Service limits

The work stays
at the office.

Ridgecourse provides clerical services to businesses. It holds neither a contractor license nor an insurance adjuster license. The purpose of the assignment determines whether we can accept it.

  1. In scope
    Records your office already has

    We record labels, references, dates and apparent differences. For general office planning, we can also list public NOAA/NWS reports by county and date, with the original source.

  2. Insurance
    Claim-related assignments are outside the pilot

    This includes preparing supplements, compiling proof for an open or anticipated claim, contacting carriers and linking weather to damage at a property. We do not solicit policyholders or accept fees tied to recoveries.

  3. Field work
    The contractor runs the job

    Inspections, measurements, bids, repairs, permit applications, crew scheduling and supervision remain with the contractor. We do not assess drying or certify completion.

  4. Documents
    The client makes legal and payment decisions

    We can record receipt of a supplied waiver or certificate. Choosing or drafting a waiver, interpreting a policy and approving a draw are outside our service.
