How it works

From scattered records
to an office handoff.

Start with a screened non-claim file or batch. Your office keeps the originals, the professional decisions and every outside communication.

  1. 01

    Screen the scope

    Tell us the business and job states, internal recipient and intended use. We screen the jurisdiction and purpose before requesting records.

  2. 02

    Set the fixed fee

    We agree on file or batch limits, outputs, delivery date, recipient and handling terms in writing.

  3. 03

    Share redacted copies

    After acceptance, use the agreed restricted folder. Keep your originals and remove unneeded personal, banking, tax and claim information.

  4. 04

    Build the index

    We copy identifiers as supplied, link matching records and flag blanks or differences. Each entry points back to its source.

  5. 05

    Resolve office questions

    Your team decides what a difference means. We can correct a transcription error or add a label your staff supplies.

  6. 06

    Hand back the file

    Your office receives the index and open-items list. We close access and delete working copies on the agreed schedule.

Service limits

The work stays
at the office.

Ridgecourse offers remote B2B administrative support. It holds neither a roofing/general-contractor license nor an insurance-adjuster license. U.S. inquiries are welcome; jurisdiction and actual file purpose determine whether work can be accepted.

  1. In scope
    Internal records your office already has

    For screened non-claim work, we record supplied labels, references, dates and literal differences. For regional planning, we can list public NOAA/NWS reports with source links.

  2. Insurance
    Claim-specific preparation is not offered

    Supplement-prep, carrier-ready packets, loss investigation, claim solicitation, negotiation and policyholder representation remain on hold pending activity-specific legal review. We do not accept fees tied to recoveries.

  3. Field work
    The licensed contractor runs the job

    Inspections, measurements, bids, repairs, permit applications, crew scheduling and supervision remain with the contractor. We do not assess drying or certify completion.

  4. Documents
    The client makes legal and payment decisions

    We can record receipt of a supplied waiver or certificate. Choosing or drafting a waiver, interpreting a policy and approving a draw are outside our service.
