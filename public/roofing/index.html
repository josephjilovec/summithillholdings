Material field guide

What’s on your roof?

The surface is a starting point. Open a material guide for questions to take to a licensed roofer. The images illustrate roof types.

Tile roofs

Clay and concrete tile cover a separate underlayment. A quote for a few broken tiles can cover very different work from a lift and reset.

Questions about tile
  • Which tiles can be reused, and how will replacements be matched?
  • Which underlayment and flashing sections are included?
  • How will hidden deck damage be recorded and priced?

Shingle roofs

Overlapping shingle courses shed water. The estimate should also account for the less visible work at edges, valleys and roof penetrations.

Questions about shingles
  • Is removal of existing layers included?
  • Which starter, edge, valley and ridge materials are specified?
  • Who documents deck condition after tear-off?

Low-slope roofs

Water needs a clear route to an outlet. Before comparing a coating with a replacement, ask how the existing roof and its drainage were assessed.

Questions about low slope
  • What roof system is currently in place?
  • How were moisture, drains and wall transitions assessed?
  • Which preparation, repairs and finish are in the price?

Reading a proposal

Compare the work
behind the price.

Two bids can describe the same roof and include different jobs. Ask both contractors to put these four answers in writing.

Take these questions with you
What comes off and what goes back?

Compare the areas measured, tear-off, disposal, product names, underlayment and edge details. Ask each bidder to name anything they expect you to arrange separately.

What happens if the deck is damaged?

Ask how findings will be photographed, what unit the repair is priced in, and whose written approval is needed before extra work begins.

When are payments due?

Pair each payment with a written milestone. Check how delays, weather interruptions and permit responsibilities are handled.

What will I have at handover?

Ask for completion photos, manufacturer and workmanship terms, applicable inspection records, and a contact for a callback. Check whose name appears on each document.
