Roofing job-file & supplement-prep audits

Water-mitigation & psychrometric log QA

Missing daily moisture readings, unlabeled affected-room photos, or mismatched dehumidifier/air-mover day counts stall mitigation billing. We audit technician-supplied logs for missing entries and assemble clean room-by-room file indexes before your billing desk invoices.

  • Daily psychrometric and moisture-reading matrix check flagging skipped days, missing dry-standard benchmarks, or unlabeled chambers.

03 · Interactive job-file & scope auditor

Audit job files before
billing stalls.

Missing deck photos, unverified supplier drop quantities, skipped drying-log days, and unsigned lien waivers are the #1 cause of delayed contractor receivables. Benchmark up to two active job files (File A vs. File B) across our 12-point documentation standard below.

Why audit photos & invoices before final billing?

Once tiles, shingles, or drywall close up a job, proving bare-deck rot, replaced W-valley metal, or extra drying days becomes impossible without timestamped mid-job photos matched cleanly to material receipts.

What triggers water-mitigation file rejections?

Gaps in daily psychrometric readings, missing dry-standard reference baselines, unlogged equipment pickup dates, and unindexed chamber photos routinely push billing cycles out by weeks.

Why track permits, COIs & lien waivers per job?

An expired subcontractor Workers’ Comp certificate or uncollected supplier lien waiver exposes a licensed contractor to audit back-charges and disputed final draws.

Who owns final estimate & billing decisions?

Always the licensed client contractor. Ridgecourse surfaces factual file gaps and invoice variances strictly for your qualified PM or estimator to verify and act on.
