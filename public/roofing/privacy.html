Privacy notice

You can use the office checklist and download a worksheet without an account. The pilot concerns ordinary, non-claim records for Arizona businesses.

Worksheet entries

The checklist runs in your browser tab. Its selections and notes are used only to display, print or download your worksheet. The site does not transmit them or save them in cookies or browser storage. A downloaded copy stays where you choose to save it.

Hosting

Images, fonts and scripts are served with the site. The hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, browser and page path to deliver and secure the website. The checklist contains no advertising pixels or third-party analytics.

Inquiries and client records

The pilot inquiry opens the Summit Hill Holdings contact form. Submitted business contact details and messages are processed to respond. Email works the same way. Share a business state, job state and general task only. Leave out customer names, addresses, tax forms, bank details, policy numbers, claim records and job photos.

If both parties agree to a pilot, the written scope identifies the approved recipient, restricted exchange method and deletion schedule. Supply only authorized, redacted business records through that channel.
