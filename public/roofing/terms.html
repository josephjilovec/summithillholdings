Ridgecourse Roofing

Terms of use

Ridgecourse Roofing offers remote clerical support for roofing and restoration businesses. The initial pilot covers Arizona businesses and Arizona projects. The work consists of organizing existing records for the client’s own office.

Scope of the service

Ridgecourse holds neither a contractor license nor an insurance adjuster license. We do not perform or bid on roof work, inspect buildings, supervise crews, prepare estimates or act for an owner on a construction project. We decline assignments intended for an existing or anticipated insurance claim, including supplements, loss investigation, damage attribution and carrier communication.

We record supplied filenames, dates, IDs and document differences. The client decides technical adequacy, billing, insurance coverage, waiver choice or validity, and permit status. A county weather reference describes a public report. It provides no determination about any individual property.

Pilots and fixed fees

A pilot starts only after the contracting parties agree in writing on the scope, fee, data handling, recipient and delivery terms. Fees are independent of insurance outcomes. Other U.S. locations may inquire about availability. An inquiry or worksheet creates no engagement.

Website tools and imagery

The worksheet lists the records a visitor marks. A checked row confirms neither job completion nor readiness to invoice. All architectural and workflow images are AI-generated illustrations.

Business inquiries
