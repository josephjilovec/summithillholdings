Terms of use

Ridgecourse Roofing is a Summit Hill Holdings venture in pre-launch. Not a Licensed Contractor. This website provides general Arizona residential roofing education, a proposal scope comparison checklist, and a personal visit worksheet. It does not offer or contract for construction, roofing, inspection-for-hire, or insurance adjusting services prior to active Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC) licensure.

Educational guide & no contracting relationship

Use the roof anatomy notes, proposal auditor, and downloaded checklists solely to inform conversations with a licensed Arizona roofing contractor of your choice. Visiting this website, generating a worksheet, or sending a general inquiry to Summit Hill Holdings does not create a contracting relationship or reserve an inspection, price, start date, or warranty.

Illustrative imagery & local code verification

All architectural roof photographs and material cutaways shown on this website are original AI-generated illustrative visuals depicting typical Sonoran Desert roof assemblies and materials. They do not depict completed customer jobs. Roof assembly specifications, manufacturer instructions, HOA rules, and municipal building permit requirements vary by property address and must be verified by an actively licensed Arizona ROC contractor and the local building department.

Finding a licensed Arizona contractor today

If your home needs active roof inspection, repair, or replacement today, verify an actively licensed R-42 or CR-42 contractor directly through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors public directory.
