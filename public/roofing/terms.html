Ridgecourse Roofing

Terms of use

Ridgecourse offers remote B2B administrative support to licensed roofing contractors, water-mitigation and fire/contents restoration companies, and multi-site commercial vendors in the United States. We accept inquiries nationally. Any engagement depends on the actual jurisdictions, purpose, written scope and authorized data route.

Scope of the service

Ridgecourse holds neither a roofing/general-contractor license nor a public-adjuster license. We do not perform or bid on roof work, inspect buildings for homeowners, supervise crews, prepare construction estimates, waive deductibles, solicit claims, investigate losses, negotiate with insurers or represent policyholders. Claim-specific supplement preparation or carrier-ready packets are not offered under the present service.

For a screened internal non-claim file, we may index supplied photo filenames and timestamps, match existing source identifiers, flag literal quantity differences and missing daily-log entries, and track the receipt or printed expiry of client-selected permit, vendor and closeout records. The client decides technical adequacy, billable amounts, insurance coverage, waiver choice or validity, permit status and job completion. A NOAA/NWS regional report does not establish damage at any individual property.

Scopes and fixed fees

An engagement starts only after the contracting parties agree in writing on eligible work, jurisdictions, fixed per-file, per-batch or monthly office-capacity fee, data handling, recipient and delivery terms. No fee is a percentage of a claim, supplement, recovery or construction value; none is contingent on an insurance outcome. An inquiry or browser worksheet creates no engagement.

Website tools and imagery

The browser-local worksheet lists the source records a visitor marks. A checked row confirms neither job completion, invoice readiness nor claim adequacy. All architectural and workflow images are AI-generated illustrations, not completed Ridgecourse customer jobs.

Business inquiries

Use the B2B inquiry form or email contact@summithillholdings.com. Send only business contact details, locations and a general description of the office task. Do not send job files, claim records, customer details or tax forms through the public inquiry.
