Terms of use

Not a Licensed Roofing Contractor & Not a Public Adjuster. Ridgecourse Roofing provides remote, fixed-fee B2B back-office operations, photo/evidence indexing, estimate-to-invoice reconciliation, permit/COI/lien-waiver tracking, and public NOAA/NWS weather-event documentation strictly to licensed contractors, restoration firms, licensed adjusters, and commercial/HOA asset teams across the United States.

Strict B2B technical-assistance boundaries

Ridgecourse Roofing does not perform roof installations, physical roof repairs, homeowner roof inspections, construction contracting, insurance claim negotiation, policy interpretation, policyholder representation, claim solicitation, or deductible waivers. All file audits, exception logs, and closeout binders are delivered exclusively to the client business’s qualified project manager or estimator, who retains sole legal and professional responsibility for licensing, estimating, billing, permits, and third-party communications.

Fixed-fee pricing only

Services are offered exclusively under written B2B Statements of Work on fixed per-file, per-batch, or fixed monthly operational capacity fees. Ridgecourse Roofing never charges contingency fees or any percentage of insurance claim proceeds. Visiting this website, using the browser-local checklist, or submitting an inquiry does not establish a client agreement.

Illustrative imagery & public weather data

All architectural photographs and workflow images displayed on this website are original AI-generated illustrative visuals. They do not depict completed customer jobs or live client files. Any storm-event compilation references publicly available NOAA/NWS records for informational organization only and does not constitute meteorological certification, engineering advice, or legal advice.
